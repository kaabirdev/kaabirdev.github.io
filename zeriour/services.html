Inside the network

What actually happens after pickup.

A scan at every handover keeps the timeline honest — for you, your customer and our operations team.

1 · Pickup

A rider collects from your address inside the booked window. The parcel is weighed and scanned to your merchant account.

2 · Sortation

Origin hub checks the label, sorts by destination district and seals the parcel into a numbered consignment bag.

3 · Line-haul

Scheduled trucks move bags between hubs overnight. Every bag scan updates all parcels inside it at once.

4 · Last mile

The destination hub assigns a rider, who attempts delivery, collects COD and closes the task with proof in the app.
