Weight & dimensions

Charges are based on actual or volumetric weight, whichever is higher. The first kilogram is included in the base rate; each additional kilogram adds ৳20.

Remote districts

COD fee

A 1.2% collection fee applies to cash-on-delivery orders, with a ৳10 minimum. Non-COD parcels have no collection fee.
