How it works

From order to doorstep in four moves.

No spreadsheets, no phone-chain guesswork. Every handover is scanned and visible to you and your customer.

01

Create your account

Sign up in minutes with your business and pickup details. Demo accounts are ready immediately.

02

Add parcels

Enter one parcel, many at once, or import a CSV. Charges and COD fees are calculated before you submit.

03

We pick up & sort

A rider collects from your address. Hubs scan, bag and dispatch on the next available manifest.

04

Delivered, COD settled

The rider closes the task, your customer gets proof of delivery, and your wallet is credited.
